# 📊 Project 1 — Revenue Integrity & Performance Analysis

![Revenue Integrity Dashboard](outputs/p1_dashboard.png)

## Executive Overview

This project simulates a real-world revenue integrity engagement for a hospitality and casino operation. Using Python, SQL, Power BI, and Excel, I developed an end-to-end analytics workflow to validate daily revenue data, identify reporting exceptions, and deliver an executive dashboard that supports operational decision-making.

The project demonstrates a complete analytics process from raw data generation and validation to business reporting and actionable recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Revenue reporting is only valuable when leadership can trust the underlying data.

Daily operational reports are often affected by duplicate transactions, missing reporting days, negative adjustments, and inconsistent departmental reporting. Left undetected, these issues can distort financial performance, reduce management confidence, and create operational risk.

This project builds a repeatable revenue validation process that identifies data quality issues before they reach executive reporting.

---

## Business Questions

This analysis answers several key business questions:

- Are there missing reporting days or departments?
- Which departments generate the highest revenue?
- Are duplicate transactions affecting reported revenue?
- Which days contain abnormal revenue spikes or declines?
- What operational insights can management use to improve reporting accuracy?

---

## Dashboard KPIs

The executive dashboard tracks:

- Total Revenue
- Average Daily Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Revenue by Department
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Data Quality Exceptions

---

## Executive Summary

After validating and cleaning the dataset, I developed an executive Power BI dashboard that provides management with a reliable view of revenue performance across multiple operating departments.

The analysis identified duplicate records, missing reporting periods, and negative revenue adjustments that would have affected financial reporting. These issues were isolated, documented, and removed before dashboard development.

With clean data, leadership can confidently evaluate departmental performance, identify operational trends, and monitor revenue activity over time.
